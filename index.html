Offline Emergency Kit

Available without internet — Essential guides for survival in coastal disasters

Works Offline — No Internet Required
🩺

First Aid Guide

▼
💉 CPR — 30 compressions, 2 breaths. Rate: 100/min
🩸 Bleeding Control — Apply pressure with clean cloth for 10+ mins
🦴 Fracture Care — Immobilize, do not move victim
🌡️ Heat Stroke — Move to shade, cool with water, call 108
🚰 Dehydration — ORS solution: 1L water + 6 tsp sugar + ½ tsp salt
🌀

Cyclone Safety

▼
🏠 Move to a sturdy building immediately
🪟 Stay away from windows and glass
📻 Listen to All India Radio for updates
🚗 Do not drive during cyclone
📦 Keep 3-day emergency supply ready
🌊

Tsunami Safety

▼
⛰️ Move to high ground immediately after earthquake
🚶 Walk/run — don't wait for vehicle
🌊 Stay 1km from coast for 24 hours after warning
📡 Wait for official all-clear signal
🏊 Never go to beach to watch tsunami waves
🌧️

Flood Safety

▼
🏔️ Move to upper floors — never enter floodwater
⚡ Turn off electricity before evacuating
🍶 Store clean drinking water
🐍 Watch for snakes in floodwater
📱 Keep phone charged and emergency contacts saved
